Common Questions

Can you help register my startup?

Absolutely. We handle the full incorporation process for Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, and Section 8 entities, including PAN, TAN, GST, and sectoral registrations.

Do you handle UPRERA registrations?

Yes. We assist real estate agents and developers with UPRERA agent registration, project registration, and ongoing quarterly compliance filings.

Can you file my GST returns?

Our in-house CA team manages GST registration, monthly and quarterly filings, and handles GST notices and assessments on your behalf.

Do you handle NRI legal matters?

Contact the chambers with a brief overview of your matter, including where it arose and any upcoming deadlines, so we can assess whether we can assist.

Do you handle matrimonial and divorce cases?

Our family law team handles divorce, legal separation, child custody, maintenance, and domestic violence matters with sensitivity and strategic precision.

What is the Virtual CFO service?

Virtual CFO provides growing businesses with strategic financial leadership on a retainer basis—covering budgeting, audit oversight, investor relations, and financial reporting.
